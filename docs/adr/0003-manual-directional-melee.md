# Manual directional power with transient charge

The leader's held nearest-target autoattack bypassed aiming, recovery and charge decisions. Use facing-directed manual release swings, keep the existing recovery timer as the authoritative 0–100% power state, and keep one bounded transient held-charge latch outside saved campaign progress. Companion AI may choose its own facing but calls the same damage operation. Existing v1 saves remain compatible and restore without a held attack.
