# Glossary
- **Traveler**: One of the three companions restoring the forest.
- **Leader**: The living traveler currently under direct control.
- **Waystone**: A refuge that replenishes the party and remembers their return point.
- **Lantern ember**: A fragment of forest light earned by calming a regional guardian.
- **Recovery**: The interval after a strike before another full attack is ready.
- **Telegraph**: A visible warning of where an enemy's next strike will land.
- **Fallen**: A traveler with no health who can return through healing or a waystone.
- **Hush**: The silence consuming the Heartroot.
